What This System Does

Comprehensive AI-powered customer analytics and retention engine

📊 Smart Prediction

Predicts whether a customer is likely to continue or leave, along with churn probability, risk level, and confidence score.

🧠 Explainable AI

Explains why churn is predicted using customer behavior patterns instead of acting as a black-box model.

💡 Decision Support

Simulates retention actions and shows how churn probability can be reduced before taking real-world decisions.

Computes Customer Lifetime Value, retention budgets, churn costs, and recommends optimal strategies per customer tier.

Auto-generates personalized offers and loyalty rewards based on churn probability and customer usage patterns.

Real-time dashboard with churn trends, risk distribution, probability spread, and exportable reports.
